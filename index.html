The Vault is a mirror, not a mind reader — built for reflection and a little entertainment, not therapy or advice. Your words stay on this device: they are never stored, never sent anywhere, and there is no account. The mirror's mind is a small model that downloads — a few hundred megabytes — and stays in your browser's cache, so after the first download the Vault works offline. Browsers sometimes clear that cache without asking — if yours does, the model downloads again, and it's quick. On iPhone, use Add to Home Screen so the download isn't cleared away. Hit PURGE (top) or close the tab and everything you said is gone.
